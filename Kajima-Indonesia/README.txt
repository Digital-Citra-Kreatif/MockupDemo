PT KAJIMA INDONESIA · WEBSITE REDESIGN · FINAL (updated 2 Nov 2026)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip / copy the whole "Kajima-Indonesia-Final" folder (keep the folders together).
2. Double-click index.html: that is the website (homepage). It opens in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
   It works offline: fonts, images, scripts and map data are all inside this folder.

design-system/design-system.html  Design system v2: foundations, components, states, do & don't
docs/                             Step documents (Markdown text) and the moodboard image
assets/ fonts/ vendor/            Files the website needs (do not move or rename)

NOTES
- Links to other Kajima group websites, Google Maps, email and phone need internet.
- On phones, open the files with a file manager that can show HTML in the browser.
- Client logos are placeholders; some photos must be replaced with approved assets.
- "Reduce motion" on the device turns the animations off; the pages still work.
