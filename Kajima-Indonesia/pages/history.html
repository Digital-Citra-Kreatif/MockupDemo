History KAJIMA CORPORATION

Since 1840

We are the top construction and architect worldwide service provider, building trust and track records that have made it one of the most highly regarded general contractors.

Milestones

  1. 1840

    Kajima begins a carpentry business in Tokyo

  2. 1860

    Pioneers the first western-style building in Yokohama (Ei-chiban Kan)

    Photo
  3. 1924

    Completes Japan's first concrete dam (Ohmine-Dam)

  4. 1930

    Kajima becomes an incorporated company

  5. 1949

    Founded Kajima Technical Research Institute (The first research facility in Japan's construction industry)

  6. 1964

    Established Kajima International Incorporated (KLL) in Los Angeles, CA, U.S.A.

  7. 1968

    Completes Japan's first high-rise building (Kasumigaseki Building)

    Photo
  8. 1983

    Completes Kawaji Dam

    Photo
  9. 1984

    Completes New Kokugikan Sumo Arena

  10. 1987

    Established Kajima Europe B.V. (KE) in the Netherlands

  11. 1988

    Established Kajima Overseas Asia Pte Ltd (KOA) in Singapore. Completes Seikan Tunnel (The world's longest undersea tunnel)

  12. 2009

    Celebrates 170th anniversary of establishment

  13. 2012

    Completes Preservation and Restoration of Tokyo Station Marunouchi Building

    Photo
  14. 2013

    Roppongi Hills

    Photo
  15. 2015

    Himeji Castle Renovation

    Photo
  16. 2019

    Nihonbashi Muromachi Mitsui Tower
